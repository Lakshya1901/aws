AnnaSetu · case study · one Delhi farm

Three months of 2026 on an 8-acre farm near Narela

An 8-acre vegetable farm near Narela, north-west Delhi: tomato on 3 acres, bottle gourd on 2, okra on 2 from mid-April and cauliflower on 1 in March. Every picking went through AnnaSetu on that day's real mandi data. The nearest mandi reporting these crops is Azadpur, 17 km away.

Result

Money AnnaSetu added, day by day

Problem classification

What was wrong at the nearest mandi

Each picking day is classed by Azadpur's state that day. Okra met 5 arrival-glut and 4 watch days after mid-April and tomato 10 watch days; the rest were normal or Azadpur had no recent report.

Verification

Did the advised market really pay more?

Out-of-sample check on every load sent away from the nearest mandi: the advised and the nearest market's next real price report (within 7 days after the decision), minus freight. Above the zero line, the advice held. The diagonal is a perfect prediction.

The direction held on most loads; the size of the gain was smaller than predicted for okra and bottle gourd. Cauliflower in March is the weak spot.

Result · rescue

Unsold stock at Azadpur found a buyer every time

Once a week about 300 kg of tomato came back unsold from Azadpur, a day after picking. The engine proposed the spoiled share from that day's temperature and sent the edible part to the best-paying mandi within 100 km.
